I got into crochet doing amigurumi, which is just using single crochet to build up 3d objects. It's fascinating that you can embed structural memory into yarn with just stitchwork and build complex 3D designs. Right now I just make the occasional tiny plushie, but eventually, I will be making [topological crochet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crocheting_Adventures_with_Hyperbolic_Planes) based off Daina Taimiņa's book as well as any [other](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10597v1) [sources](https://ncartmuseum.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Reef-How-To-FINAL2020-copy-1.pdf) I can find. My notes on that work will go here.

I think most people, though, practice crochet for the same reason they'd knit: to make comfy, beautiful clothes. Crochet is slower and needs more yarn, but you can get crazy with the textures you can make in the fabric. To get myself to write, I've started a mini-project of [[Crochet/Crochet Stitches/map|documenting]] these stitches and the fabrics you can make with them.
