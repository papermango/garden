In my senior year of college, I served as one of the organizers of the UIUC Coffee Club, and handled water chemistry, pour-over brewing, and an espresso offering. Back then, I used a 4:6 method to brew our pour overs and the [[South Indian coffee filter]] to brew the espresso, and insisted on 30:10 water.

I've long since changed these methods, but the love for coffee has remained.
