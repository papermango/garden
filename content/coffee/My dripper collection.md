Over the years, I've built up a frankly ridiculous collection of drippers all dedicated to making pour-over coffee. I'll write about each of them eventually. For now, here's a table. Inactive drippers are ones I've stopped using for whatever reason.

| Dripper                 | Material                                                                   | Active? | Shape      | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Orea V3                 | Trogamid plastic                                                           | Yes     | Flat       | I got the Ice V3 out of sheer luck when Horia dropped a batch of rejects. Easily my fastest brewer and incredibly consistent.                                                                                                                                                        |
| April                   | Makrolon polycarbonate                                                     | Yes     | Flat       | Picked mine up from Kumquat in LA. It's a beautiful brewer, but unfortunately it's prone to clogging and pretty slow to drain. It's advertised as a fast, modern third wave brewer, so I'm not sure what's up with that. Needs more experimentation since I'd love to use this more. |
| Hario V60 Neo           | Tritan plastic                                                             | Yes     | Cone       | Just picked this up! Super excited to give it a run. I'm hoping it'll fill the gap the Kono left.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Origami Air             | [Resin plastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Styrene-acrylonitrile_resin) | No      | Flat, cone | The Origami can do both flats and cones, and this one has better thermal resistance than its ceramic version. I flip between the two based on how I'm feeling that month.                                                                                                            |
| Origami S               | Ceramic                                                                    | Yes     | Flat, cone | My first ever dripper! It's technically the worse Origami compared to the Air, but right now, I like using it for the nostalgia. Still a great brewer too!                                                                                                                           |
| Kalita Wave             | Steel                                                                      | No      | Flat       | The Kalita is the archetypal flat-bottom brew and is essentially infinitely durable. I pull it out sometimes if I want sweet and non-acidic brews or if I've got a medium roast.                                                                                                     |
| Hario V60, Cafec Flower | Plastic                                                                    | No      | Cone       | I've grouped these two together because after using them both for coffee club, I can confidently say they work the exact same. Unfortunately they have the exact same cheap materials and ended up fully cracking after years of thermal stress.                                     |
| Kono                    | Plastic                                                                    | No      | Cone       | I really wanted to like this one. It's the most consistent cone-shaped brewer I've used thanks to the low bypass design. Unfortunately its materials are far worse than the price tag suggests, and both of mine ended up irreparably cracked.[^1]                                   |
| Hario V60 (Switch)      | Glass                                                                      | Yes     | Cone       | This one's kinda cheating b/c it's a two-in-one; the Switch base can detach and attach to other brewers, notably the Neo. Without its base it's a glass V60, which works just as well as you'd expect.                                                                               |

[^1]: After losing four brewers to thermal stress cracks, I'll be avoiding cheap plastics in my brewers going forward. Yes, the brewers work just as well with or without cracks; I don't like feeling like I have microplastics in my brew.
